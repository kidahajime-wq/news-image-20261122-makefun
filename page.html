CLIMATE  •  ANTALYA, TURKEY

Climate talks run into a second day of overtime — and fossil fuels vanish from the draft

Negotiators at the UN summit were asked on Sunday to back a last-minute deal that still does not name coal, oil or gas.

COP31 president Murat Kurum told a late-night plenary on Saturday that he believed he had “a text which all the parties can agree upon”. Delegates had until 03:00 GMT on Sunday to object, with a session to approve the deal set for 06:00 GMT.

The draft keeps a goal to lift electricity’s share of final energy use to 35% by 2035 and backs carbon capture with a target of 1 billion tonnes.
