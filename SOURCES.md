## Lead — COP31 climate talks, Antalya

- Al Jazeera, 22 Nov 2026: "Fossil fuel impasse pushes COP31 climate talks
  into second day of overtime" — COP31 president Murat Kurum's late-night
  Saturday plenary, objections due 03:00 GMT Sunday, approval session 06:00
  GMT; talks due to end Friday.
- Channel News Asia, 22 Nov 2026: "Fossil fuels still missing from latest
  COP31 draft deal" — the presidency's Sunday proposal again fails to mention
  fossil fuels by name; needs consensus from nearly 200 nations.

  contains none of the words "fossil", "oil", "coal" or "phase out"; it sets a
  goal of 35% electricity share of final energy by 2035 and 1 billion tonnes
  of carbon capture and storage.

## EU–Indonesia trade deal

- Reuters, 21 Nov 2026: the EU and Indonesia signed the Comprehensive
  Economic Partnership Agreement and an Investment Protection Agreement in
  Brussels after 10 years of negotiations; tariffs removed on more than 90%
  of products. Reported through Sunday 22 Nov (SANA; Rapporteur, updated
  22 Nov): tariffs scrapped on about 98% of EU exports to Indonesia; in
  force in early 2027 after ratification; a market of about 730 million
  people.
